01· The contest

Five days.
One seat each.

Teams of any size take one open role and implement it against its published contract. There is no track, no theme, and no pitch. A seat is filled when something runs and someone else can point their client at it.

03· Book a place

One conversation.
No guest list.

Pick a time and talk to Rinat before the week starts — who you are, which seat interests you, and whether coming is worth it for you. No token, no fee, and no waiting list to be ranked on.

The form asks for a phone number. Any number will do.

Open the booking page →

The form is hosted by Cal.com. Choosing a slot gives them your name, your email and whatever you type in the phone field, and puts the call on our calendar — nothing else is recorded.

A conversation is not a selection.
